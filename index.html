Clubs In Belize High School

Belize High School is an established private school located in Belize City, Belize, offering education for students typically from ages 12 to 18.



Hello Students!

Our school offers a variety of clubs that allow students to explore their interests, develop new skills, and meet other students. From Robotics and Coding to Sports and Chess, there is a club for students with different interests and talents. These clubs encourage creativity, teamwork, problem-solving, and personal growth outside the classroom.
