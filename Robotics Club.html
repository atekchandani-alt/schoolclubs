"Robotics Club"

"The Robotics Club is a hands-on club where students explore robotics, engineering, and technology. Members work together to design, build, and program robots while developing creativity, problem-solving, and teamwork skills."

Purposes and Activities Offered

The purpose of a robotics club is to provide students with hands-on learning experiences that combine engineering, coding, and creativity.

Robotics clubs often start with practical, hands-on projects to introduce students to robotics concepts.

Club Advisor And Requirements

Mr. Daniel Martinez, guides students through robotics projects and helps them develop engineering, teamwork, and problem-solving skills.

Must be interested in robotics and technology. Attend regular club meetings. Be willing to work in a team. Participate in club projects and activities.

Robot Building

Members work together to design and build robots using different materials, electronic components, and simple mechanical parts. This activity allows students to turn their ideas into working machines while developing creativity and engineering skills.

Coding Challenges

Students learn how to program robots to perform different tasks and respond to challenges. Through these activities, members practice logical thinking, problem-solving, and basic programming while working together to find solutions.

Robotics Competition

Members participate in friendly robotics competitions where they test the robots they have designed and programmed. These events give students a chance to apply what they have learned while developing teamwork, communication, and problem-solving skills.
